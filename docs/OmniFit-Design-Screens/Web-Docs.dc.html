Getting started

Create your coach account

Setting up OmniFit takes about five minutes. This guide walks through creating your account, choosing a plan, and getting your profile ready before you invite your first client.

1. Sign up with your email

Head to the sign-up page and enter your name, email, and a password. You'll start on a 14-day free trial with no credit card required — you can explore every feature before choosing a plan.

2. Build your public profile

Add a photo, your specialties, and a short bio. This is what prospective clients see when they find you through Find a Coach or a direct link, so it's worth spending a few minutes getting right.

3. Set your availability & session types

Define the session types you offer — strength training, check-in calls, form review — and the times you're generally available. Clients will request sessions against this when booking.

Tip: You can always adjust your session types and availability later from Settings → Plans.
