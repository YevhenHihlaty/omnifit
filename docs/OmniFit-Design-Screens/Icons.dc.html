Icon pack

62 line icons covering meals & nutrition, training types, body metrics, navigation, coaching and status states used across the app. 24×24 grid, 1.8px rounded stroke, currentColor so every icon inherits its context's color — a few badge icons (star, streak, new) are solid-filled instead. Downloadable as individual SVGs, a symbol sprite, and a React-ready pack.
