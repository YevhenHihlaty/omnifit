Logo & mark

The mark has to do two things at once: show the bond between a coach and a client, and hint at what the product gives back — a practice that holds together, not just contact. Orbit draws both in a single shape: a ring for the coach's practice, a point for the client moving through it, always in view.

Four directions

Ascent Mark
Two points, one line rising between them — reads clearly at a glance, but leans more "chart" than "practice."
Bridge Mark
An arch joining two sides — reads as partnership and trust, but says nothing about outcomes.
Recommended
Orbit Mark
A client held inside the coach's practice — the ring is the practice, the point is the client, always in view.
OmniFit
Wordmark only
Kept as the minimal fallback lockup for narrow spaces — not the primary.

The ring is the coach's practice — steady, always there, holding its shape. The point is the client, drawn close and kept in view rather than left to drift. One shape, two audiences — a client sees themselves held in someone's orbit; a coach sees their whole practice as one continuous thing instead of scattered spreadsheets and chats.

Clearspace & minimum size

Clearspace on every side ≥ the radius of one end dot. Minimum digital height: 22px. Minimum print height: 9mm.

Misuse
