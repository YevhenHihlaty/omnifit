1. Introduction

OmniFit ("we," "us," or "our") provides software that connects independent fitness coaches with their clients. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through the OmniFit web and mobile applications (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices available to coaches and clients. By creating an account or accepting a coach's invitation, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect account information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password. Coaches may additionally provide business information such as a bio, certifications, and payout details. Clients who join through an invite may provide onboarding details such as height, weight, training goals, and progress photos or measurements they choose to log.

We also collect information generated through your use of the Service, including workout logs, messages exchanged with your coach, session bookings, and device or usage data such as browser type and IP address, for security and product-improvement purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the Service, including to deliver coaching content assigned by a coach, facilitate scheduling and messaging between a coach and their clients, process coach payouts, send account and product notifications, and maintain the security and integrity of the platform.

4. Data Sharing with Your Coach

Because OmniFit is a coaching relationship platform, information a client enters — including workout logs, measurements, and messages — is visible to the coach who invited them. Clients should review information they share with this in mind. OmniFit does not sell client or coach personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and activity data for as long as an account remains active, and for a limited period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. A coach or client may request deletion of their account as described in Section 6.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You may exercise these rights by contacting us using the information in Section 9. We will respond to verified requests within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify coaches and clients by email or through an in-product notice before the changes take effect.
