# OmniFit — Business Plan (MVP)

> **September 2026** · Canonical detail: [`feature-overview-for-coaches-and-partners.md`](feature-overview-for-coaches-and-partners.md) · Engineering: [`DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md`](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md)

## One-line pitch

**One app where an independent coach runs their entire client roster** — invites, scheduling, logs, progress photos, and messaging — without gyms, facilities, or studio admin in v1.

## Problem

Solo coaches stitch together WhatsApp, spreadsheets, Calendly, MyFitnessPal, Drive, and Zoom. Nothing holds **all clients** and **shared history** in one place.

## Solution (MVP)

| Who | What they get |
|-----|----------------|
| **Coach (customer)** | Sign up → invite clients by email → **My Clients** list → calendar, analytics, log-on-behalf, tasks, chat |
| **Client (free)** | Accept invite → log meals, training, habits, body + photos → book sessions with **their** coach |

## What we are not building in MVP

- Gym / facility / venue catalogs and booking ops  
- Public **Find Coach** marketplace (Phase 2)  
- **Team** multi-coach studio workspace (Phase 3+)  

## ICP

Independent online or hybrid coaches with roughly **5–30 clients**, not gym chains or facility operators.

## Go-to-market

10 pilot coaches; each adds clients via **invite link**; iterate on roster + logging loop before discovery or studio tiers.

## Revenue (phased)

| Phase | Monetization |
|-------|----------------|
| **MVP beta** | Free tier only (cap: 3 active clients) |
| **Phase 2** | **Pro $29/mo** — unlimited clients, analytics, live video + in-call log, Stripe |
| **Phase 3+** | **Team $99/mo** for studios; gym/location catalog; partner comp |

Clients never pay.

## Success metrics (90 days)

- 50 registered coaches, 200 coach–client pairings, 30 weekly active coaches  
- Team / studio orgs tracked from Phase 3 onward (not an MVP gate)

## Competitive wedge

Replace the **six-tool stack** with one coach workspace; coach-side **progress photo gallery** and structured training log; invite-only trust until marketplace ships.
